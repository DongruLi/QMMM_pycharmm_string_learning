> What is the QM region?

In QM/MM simulations, only a small part of the system is treated with quantum mechanics (QM).
This is usually the part where the chemical reaction happens.

---

>  QM region in this tutorial

In this tutorial, we choose the **chorismate molecule (segment ID: HETA)** as the QM region.

This is because:
- the reaction happens inside the substrate
- it keeps the calculation simple and fast

---

>  Define the QM region in CHARMM

We define the QM region using the segment ID: HETA
define qm sele segid HETA end

This tells CHARMM that all atoms in segment **HETA** belong to the QM region.

---

>  Charge and spin

For the chorismate molecule:

- Total charge = **-2**
- Spin multiplicity = **1** (closed-shell system)

At this stage, you can simply use these values without worrying about the details.
In the next section, we will connect PyCHARMM with xTB to compute QM/MM energies.
